Pre-U mathematics course for international students. 

In this course, we will briefly inroduce the Chinese high school level mathematics, including basic algebra, set theory, elementary functions, derivatives, basic analytic geometry, and basic probability theory. The purpose of this course is to provide the mathematical foundation needed for the subsequent study of basic calculus.

Important Exams
====
**Standardized exams**: The results of the standardized exams determine which majors you will be eligible to choose when entering the undergraduate program. The exams are expected to take place sometime between the middle and end of next semester (about 4.20-6.10 maybe). Based on previous years, students are usually given two opportunities to take them. There are two levels, A and B. Some schools and programs with stronger requirements in mathematics and science, such as Mathematics, Information Technology, Yuanpei, and Guanghua, require students to pass the Level A exam. Students in Class B are advised to take the Level B exam on their first attempt; if they pass, they may choose to take the Level A exam on their second attempt.
